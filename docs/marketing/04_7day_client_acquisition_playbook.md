## 1. Day-by-Day Action Plan

### **Day 1: Prospect Selection & ICP Filter**
Identify 50 mid-market companies on LinkedIn Sales Navigator / Apollo that fit these criteria:
* **Company Size:** 50–500 employees (fast sales cycle vs 10,000+ corp).
* **Technographic Trigger:** Job posts mentioning *"data labeling"*, *"training data bottleneck"*, *"synthetic data"*, or *"model performance stagnation"*.
* **Verticals:** Healthcare AI, Drone Telemetry, Clinical Document Processing, Retail Demand Forecasting.

### **Day 2–3: Execute 3-Touch Cold Outreach**
* Launch personalized cold email + LinkedIn message sequence (see `05_outbound_email_and_linkedin_pitch_templates.md`).
* Offer a **Low-Friction Hook**: A free "48-Hour Sample Data Quality & Noise Audit".

### **Day 4–5: Follow-Up & Warm Nurture**
* Send case study proof points (*Healthcare VAE Synthetic MRI or Drone Telemetry Annotation*).
* Direct interested leads to the **Avora vs Incumbents** comparison page (`06_avora_vs_competitors_landing_page.md`).

### **Day 6–7: Discovery Call & Closing Script**
* Present the 5-Discipline Framework during the 30-minute call.
* Offer a 2-week paid pilot sprint ($15,000 – $30,000) to audit and remediate their data pipeline.
